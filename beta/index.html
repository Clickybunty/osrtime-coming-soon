The beta process

How it works

The beta runs through Google Play's private testing track. There is no public download link — access is enabled per Google account.

1
Request Access

Send us an email from the address linked to your Google Play account. Tell us about your use case and Android device.

2
We Review & Enable

We manually register your Google account for the private testing track. This is not automated.

3
Install via Play Store

You receive a Google Play beta opt-in link by email. Open it on your Android device and install the app from the Play Store.

4
Test in Your Workflow

Use OSRTIME Sync-Box TCR in real production. Report what works, what doesn't, and what you'd like to see next.

Apply for beta access

Who we are looking for

Testers who work in or adjacent to professional production environments and are willing to integrate OSRTIME Sync-Box TCR into a real workflow — not just a test bench.

  • Film and video production crews
  • Production sound recordists and boom operators
  • Broadcast and live production teams
  • Media technology educators and students
  • Anyone working with professional timecode equipment

No prior experience with OSRTIME is required. A working Android device and a Google Play account are sufficient.

Request Beta Access
To participate, send us an email from the Google account you use with Google Play. Include your Android device model and a brief description of your use case.
Beta access is limited and subject to availability. Access is not automatic. We review all requests manually.
Important: Use the same Google account that is signed in to the Google Play Store on your Android device. Installing through the testing track requires this account to be registered first.

Google Play: The app is not publicly available on the Play Store. The testing track link is sent to approved testers only — it cannot be accessed by unregistered accounts.
