Android App Capabilities

Professional production tools on Android

Core timecode, synchronization and recording workflows included in OSRTIME Sync-Box TCR for Android.

⏱
Timecode Generation

SMPTE timecode at 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97 NDF/DF, 30 NDF/DF and high frame rates. Time of Day, custom start value or jammed from an external source.

Timecode explained
🔊
LTC In & Out

Receive and decode Linear Timecode via built-in microphone or USB audio interface. Output LTC via audio jack or compatible interface.

How Jam Sync works
📡
Wi-Fi Synchronization

Synchronize timecode between multiple Android devices over standard Wi-Fi. Auto-Sync maintains alignment across a production fleet.

Network & Sync
🎙
Field Recording

Record audio with embedded BWF metadata including timecode, sample rate and channel assignment. 48 kHz, up to 4 channels.

Recording guide
🔌
USB Audio Routing

Class-compliant USB audio interfaces with up to 4 channels, manual 24-bit recording support on compatible hardware and per-channel metering.

Routing guide
